A five-step follow-up routine

01 · ONE INBOX

Record new enquiries

Whether the enquiry comes from a call, website, WhatsApp or a walk-in, add it to one permitted team tracker. Don't store personal details you don't need.

02 · ONE OWNER

Assign the next person

Each open enquiry needs a named staff owner. That way “we will call back” becomes an accountable action instead of an assumption.

03 · SPECIFIC NEXT STEP

Write a due date

Replace vague statuses such as “interested” with concrete permitted next steps: answer a fee question, confirm a demo or provide requested course details.

04 · SHORT DAILY REVIEW

Check what's due

At the start of the day, look at due and overdue records. Assign work, record outcomes and close enquiries that no longer require follow-up.

05 · RESPECT CHOICE

Review messages and opt-outs

Human-review every draft, confirm the learner's question and permission, and stop contact when someone declines or opts out.
