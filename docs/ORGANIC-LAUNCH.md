## Draft educational article outline
Title: 5 checks before putting an n8n lead intake webhook into production
1. Validate and normalize input.
2. Authenticate and rate-limit public endpoints.
3. Store a unique idempotency key to prevent duplicate side effects.
4. Avoid leaking customer data in logs and alerts.
5. Test retries, timeouts, and failure notifications.
