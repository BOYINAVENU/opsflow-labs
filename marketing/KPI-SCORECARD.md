# Marketing scorecard

Start with zero verified followers, leads, or sales unless source analytics prove otherwise.

Weekly metrics:
- Published educational posts by channel
- GitHub referral visits and repository stars
- Website unique visitors (requires analytics integration)
- Early-access email inquiries (requires mailbox tracking)
- Qualified customer conversations
- Paid checkouts and refund rate (requires verified processor)
- Conversion from visitor to inquiry and inquiry to purchase

Never report hypothetical results as actual metrics. Review content quality and conversion before increasing posting volume.
