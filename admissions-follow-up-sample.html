Sample 01 · New course enquiry

Reply with the answer and a next step

Hello [first name], thanks for your enquiry about [course name]. The current [batch date / course duration / fee details] are [verified details]. Would you like us to send the syllabus or help you choose a suitable batch? Regards, [staff member] | [training centre]

Use when the learner has contacted the centre and the response is within their request. Replace every placeholder; don't invent availability or pricing.

Sample 02 · Requested callback follow-up

Follow up respectfully, without pressure

Hello [first name], following up on your request for information about [course / demo]. Would the latest batch schedule or answers to your course questions be helpful? Happy to share only what you need. If you'd prefer no more follow-ups, just let us know and we'll stop. Regards, [staff member] | [training centre]

Only use when a follow-up is appropriate and permitted. Respect opt-outs immediately. Do not use this as an unsolicited bulk-message script.

Owner hand-off · One example record

Don't stop at “interested”

Record one permitted enquiry with a clear staff owner and due action. Example below is fictional; no actual student data is used.

Vague record

“Interested in data course. Call later.”

Actionable record

“Staff A owns EXAMPLE-001. Next action: answer confirmed fee questions. Due: 11 Nov. Status: waiting on staff.”

  1. Assign a named staff member.
  2. Record the specific answer or action needed.
  3. Set a due date and review open actions each working day.
  4. Record opt-outs and avoid unnecessary personal data.
